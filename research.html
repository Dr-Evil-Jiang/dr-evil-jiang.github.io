Research Topics

Research interests

My research focuses on near-field sensing and communications, pinching-antenna systems (PASS), beamforming, and machine learning for wireless networks.

  • Near-field sensing and integrated sensing and communications (ISAC)

  • Pinching-antenna systems (PASS) and segmented waveguide-enabled architectures (SWANs)

  • Multi-antenna signal processing, beamforming, beam training, and beam alignment

  • Machine learning for wireless networks, including deep learning and reinforcement learning

  • Spectrum sharing for IoT, symbiotic radio, and cross-layer PHY/MAC performance analysis

Selected topics

  • Near-field sensing and predictive beamforming: Position estimation, user tracking, active-sensing-based beam training, and predictive beamforming.

  • PASS and SWAN transceiver design: Uplink and downlink communications, tri-hybrid beamforming, integrated sensing and communications, and over-the-air computation.

  • Sensing bounds and optimization: Cramér-Rao bound analysis for continuous-aperture arrays, Bayesian Cramér-Rao bounds, and Ziv-Zakai bounds for pinching-antenna-assisted sensing.

  • Learning-based beam alignment: Online learning for MIMO fluid antenna systems and deep unfolding for near-field user tracking.

  • Covert communications: Beamforming with pinching-antenna systems and sensing-assisted design.

  • Joint communication and control: Closed-loop control beamforming and B2X networks for embodied intelligence.

  • Spectrum sharing for IoT: Transmission mode selection and fairness-aware backscatter-device scheduling in symbiotic radio networks.
