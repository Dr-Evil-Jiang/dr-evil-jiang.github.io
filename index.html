About me

Hao Jiang (Jeremy) is a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Engineering at Queen Mary University of London (QMUL), where he has passed his viva subject to minor corrections. He received his B.S. and M.S. degrees from Nankai University, China. His research focuses on near-field sensing and communications, pinching-antenna systems (PASS), beamforming, and machine learning for wireless networks.

His previous research investigated spectrum sharing between Internet of Things (IoT) devices and legacy users. He has experience in optimization, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and cross-layer performance analysis across the physical (PHY) and medium access control (MAC) layers.
