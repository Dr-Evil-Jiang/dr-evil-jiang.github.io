About me

Hao Jiang (Jeremy) is a Ph.D. candidate in Electronic Engineering at Queen Mary University of London (QMUL). He received the B.S. and M.Eng. degrees from Nankai University (NKU), China. His current research interests include reconfigurable antennas, near-field communications, and near-field sensing. Previously, he worked on spectrum sharing between Internet of Things (IoT) devices and legacy users. His expertise spans machine-learning-aided optimization (e.g., deep learning and reinforcement learning) and cross-layer performance analysis across the physical (PHY) and medium access control (MAC) layers.
